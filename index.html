Ok prompt

Great prompt

Create a personal chief-of-staff assistant that reviews my Gmail each morning, summarizes urgent messages, checks Calendar for upcoming meetings, pulls relevant Notion context, and gives me a concise action list. Do not send, delete, or schedule anything without my approval. Output as: urgent items, meeting prep, suggested replies, and follow-ups.

ABOUT SOLARFUNK LABS

Optimistic technical education for the AI era.

SolarFunk Labs is a workshop studio for people who want practical AI literacy: not hype, not doom, and not abstract theory. The goal is to help students build useful systems they understand, control, and can safely personalize.
